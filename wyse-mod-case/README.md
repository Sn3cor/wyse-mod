# Wyse converter enclosure

Two-piece enclosure for the `wyse-mod-pcb` Rev 1.0 board. The printable
parts are `base.stl` and `lid.stl`.

## Hardware

- 4 × M3 × 5 mm socket-head screws
- Assembled converter PCB with a Micro-USB Pro Micro

The screws are installed from inside the enclosure, pass through the
PCB's 3.2 mm mounting holes, and self-tap into 2.6 mm blind pilot holes
in the base bosses. After passing through the 1.6 mm PCB, each M3×5
screw has approximately 3.4 mm of engagement. The friction-fit lid hides
all four screw heads.
Start each screw gently and avoid repeatedly removing it, as printed
threads wear over time.
